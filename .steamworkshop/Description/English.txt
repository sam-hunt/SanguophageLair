Sanguophage Lair

[h1]About[/h1]
Sanguophages haunt RimWorld's lore as ancient, scheming mysteries - but in vanilla Biotech you mostly meet them alone in shuttles or rare meetings at your own colony. This mod gives them a home worth raiding: a rare world quest site concealing a true sanguophage lair.

[h2]The Lair[/h2]
[list]
[*]A rare opportunity-site quest reveals a sanguophage lair on the world map
[*]The surface site houses a lone Ancient Stairwell; the real lair lies in a generated pocket map below
[*]An ancient sanguophage and its thralls guard hemogen-soaked treasures
[*]Quest commonality is configurable in mod settings (or disable it entirely)
[/list]

[hr][/hr]

[h1]Compatibility[/h1]
[list]
[*]Requires RimWorld 1.6 and the Biotech DLC
[*]Safe to add to existing saves; not safe to remove
[*]Not tested with Combat Extended
[/list]
